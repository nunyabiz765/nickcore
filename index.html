You can watch the whole night turn. It opens hard with 2Pac and Kanye, Tears for Fears trips the switch, and from there it's a slow drift down into warm 80s synth-melancholy — two New Order appearances hours apart, unplanned. No theme decided in advance; just a man following the feeling downhill until it landed on Prefab Sprout. That's a fingerprint.
